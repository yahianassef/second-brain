What's inside

Everything you track, in one place.

Each one is a proper tool, not a placeholder — and they all share the same data, the same forms and the same sync engine, so anything you add on one device shows up on the others.

Habits · Studying · Exercising

See the month you actually had.

Syncing

Your devices, your accounts.

There is no backend to sign up for. Pick whichever path suits you — or both — and the apps stay in step item by item, with the most recently edited copy winning.

Google Sheets

A script you deploy once, under your own account, turns your spreadsheets into the app's API. Expenses, credit cards, fuel and service read and write straight to the tabs you already keep.

A private gist

No Google account needed. One secret GitHub gist keeps every device in step, merges edits per item and remembers deletions, so nothing you removed can quietly come back.

Plain files, always

A full JSON backup in one click, and a CSV per section whose columns line up with the companion workbook. Nothing is locked in a format only this app can read.

Privacy

Nothing phones home.

The app is one static file. It talks to exactly two hosts, and only the ones you connect yourself: GitHub, and your own Apps Script deployment.

No analytics

No trackers, no pixels, no telemetry of any kind.

No third-party scripts

No CDN, no fonts fetched, no frameworks. Everything is in the file.

Local first

Your data lives in your browser's storage and in whichever sync you set up.

Tokens stay put

Kept in your browser only — never committed, never sent anywhere else.

Open it and start with one thing.

A task for today, or a habit you want to keep. The rest of it will be waiting when you need it.
